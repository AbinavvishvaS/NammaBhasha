## 🌟 Why NammaBhasha is a Game Changer for Beginners

Traditional translators expect users to already know what to search, how grammar connects, and how locals speak. For a visitor, student, or migrant with **zero prior knowledge of Kannada**, dictionary translations fail in real life.

**NammaBhasha is an all-in-one survival & conversational superpower designed for complete beginners:**

## 🚀 Massive Flagship Feature Modules

### 0. 🌟 Zero-Literacy Visual Communicator (ಚಿತ್ರ ಭಾಷೆ — Tap to Talk)
> **Empowering non-readers, elders, domestic staff, and migrant workers who cannot read or write.**
* **Picture-First Interface**: 18 large, intuitive visual cards across Auto, Bus, Coffee, Food, Water, UPI QR, Hospital, SOS, and daily needs.
* **Instant Speech-on-Touch**: Simply touching any picture card causes the phone to speak loud, authentic Kannada immediately.
* **Giant Screen Mode (ಡ್ರೈವರ್‌ಗೆ ತೋರಿಸಿ)**: Tap expand to display massive 56px typography on an ultra-high-contrast card to show drivers or shopkeepers silently.
* **Visual Indian Rupee Cash & Change Calculator (ಹಣ ಎಣಿಕೆ)**: Color-coded real Indian currency note buttons (₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500). Non-readers simply tap the notes they hold to count fares or grocery totals; pressing the audio button announces the full sum aloud in natural spoken Kannada (e.g. *"ನೂರ ಅರವತ್ತು ರೂಪಾಯಿ"*), safeguarding vulnerable users against being overcharged or cheated.
* **Spoken Voice Guide (ಧ್ವನಿ ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ)**: Tap the voice guide button anytime to hear an audio explanation in simple Kannada explaining how to use the app with no reading needed!

### 1. 🧩 Smart 3-Click Sentence Builder (ವಾಕ್ಯ ರಚನೆ)
* **Zero grammar knowledge needed!** Construct authentic Kannada sentences visually.
* Simply click 3 cards:
  - **Who (Subject)**: `ನನಗೆ` (To me / I), `ನಾವು` (We), `ಇದು` (This), `ನೀವು` (You)
  - **What (Object)**: `ಒಂದು ಕಾಫಿ` (One coffee), `ಮಸಾಲೆ ದೋಸೆ` (Masala dosa), `ಬಸ್ ಟಿಕೆಟ್` (Bus ticket), `ನೀರು` (Water), `ಮೆಟ್ರೋ ಸ್ಟೇಷನ್` (Metro station), `ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ` (Hospital), `ಬಿಲ್` (Bill)
  - **Action / Request**: `ಬೇಕು` (Want), `ಬೇಡ` (Don't want), `ಕೊಡಿ` (Please give), `ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?` (Where is it?), `ಎಷ್ಟು ರೂಪಾಯಿ?` (How much?)
* Instantly generates the combined Kannada phrase, Latin phonetic guide, English meaning, and **one-tap audio playback & full-screen display for locals!**

### 2. 🛡️ Newcomer Town Survival Hub & Auto Fare Shield (ನಗರ ಸರ್ವೈವಲ್ ಕಿಟ್)
> **Engineered specifically for non-Kannadiga newcomers, students, and workers to survive in any Karnataka town or city with zero Kannada knowledge.**
* **Tension Diffuser & Golden Lifelines (ವಿವಾದ ನಿವಾರಣೆ)**:
  - *"ಕನ್ನಡ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಗೊತ್ತು, ಕಲಿತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ ಅಣ್ಣ!"* ("I know a little Kannada, I am learning brother!") — Instantly melts awkwardness and turns tense situations into warm, brotherly support.
  - Conflict diffusers, polite English/Hindi transition requests, and respectful addressing using **"Anna" (Brother)** and **"Akka" (Sister)**.
* **1-Touch Street Direction & Voice Stop Pad (ದಾರಿ & ಸ್ಟಾಪ್ ಕಮಾಂಡ್‌ಗಳು)**:
  - Instant voice command buttons: 🛑 *"ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ ಅಣ್ಣ!"* (Stop right here!), ⬆️ *"ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಮುಂದೆ ಹೋಗಿ"* (Go straight), ⬅️ *"ಮುಂದೆ ಎಡಗಡೆ ತಿರುಗಿ"* (Turn left), ➡️ *"ಮುಂದೆ ಬಲಗಡೆ ತಿರುಗಿ"* (Turn right), 📍 *"ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ?"* (How far?), 🚏 *"ಯಾವ ಬಸ್ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೆ?"* (Which bus goes?).
* **Karnataka Auto Meter Defender & RTO Estimator (ಆಟೋ ಮೀಟರ್ ರಕ್ಷಣೆ)**:
  - Prevents non-Kannadigas from getting overcharged: calculates official Karnataka RTO fares (₹30 for first 2 km, ₹15/km thereafter, +50% night rate from 10 PM to 5 AM).
  - 1-Touch button to **speak estimated fare aloud in Kannada to the driver** or **show a giant high-contrast fare card**, plus the essential *"ಮೀಟರ್ ಹಾಕಿ ಸ್ವಾಮಿ (Please put meter)"* audio reminder.
* **Darshini Dining Protocol & Spice Defense (ದರ್ಶಿನಿ ಊಟ)**:
  - Token counter navigation, *"ಖಾರ ಬೇಡ / ಖಾರ ಕಮ್ಮಿ ಮಾಡಿ"* (No spicy / mild), *"ಬಿಸಿ ನೀರು ಕೊಡಿ"* (Hot drinking water), *"ಚಟ್ನಿ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಜಾಸ್ತಿ ಕೊಡಿ"* (Extra chutney), and *"ಪಾರ್ಸೆಲ್ ಕಟ್ಟಿ"* (Takeaway).
* **5 Golden Cultural Rules for Outsiders (ನಗರ ಸಭ್ಯತೆಯ ೫ ಗೋಲ್ಡನ್ ರೂಲ್ಸ್)**:
  - 5 core etiquette practices (Addressing as Anna/Akka, mastering the multi-purpose word "Aaythu", UPI readiness, auto meter habits, and showing 5% language effort) with audio narration.
* **Interactive Alphabet Soundboard & Numbers**:
  - Soundboard (`ಅ, ಆ, ಇ, ಈ...`) and currency counter (1 to 1000) with Kannada script and numerals (`೧, ೨, ೩...`).
* **10 Magic Words**: Quick cheat cards for essential daily words (`ಹೌದು, ಇಲ್ಲ, ಬೇಕು, ಬೇಡ, ಎಷ್ಟು, ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ, ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ, ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು, ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು, ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ`).

### 3. 🔥 Bangalore Slang & Colloquial Hub (ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು ಕನ್ನಡ 101)

  - **Scene Illa (ಸೀನ್ ಇಲ್ಲ)**: No problem / chill
  - **Swamy (ಸ್ವಾಮಿ)**: Respectful address for elders & drivers
  - **Anna / Akka (ಅಣ್ಣ / ಅಕ್ಕ)**: Universal friendly honorific

### 4. 🏙️ 10 Real-Life Situational Categories
1. **Travel & Commute (ಪ್ರಯಾಣ)**: BMTC buses, metro directions, autos, and airport shuttles.
2. **Food & Darshini (ಊಟ ಮತ್ತು ತಿಂಡಿ)**: Taaza Thindi/CTR dosa orders, filter coffee, token counters, and spicy levels (`ಖಾರ ಕಮ್ಮಿ ಮಾಡಿ`).
3. **Shopping & Markets (ಖರೀದಿ)**: KR Market bargaining, fresh vegetables, change (`ಚಿಲ್ಲರೆ`), and UPI QR codes.
4. **Healthcare & Clinic (ಆರೋಗ್ಯ)**: Symptoms, consulting doctors, medication timing, and emergency care.
5. **House & PG Renting (ಮನೆ ಮತ್ತು ಬಾಡಿಗೆ)**: To-let boards, rent, advance deposits, Cauvery water, and EB bills.
6. **Workplace & Office (ಕೆಲಸದ ಸ್ಥಳ)**: Meeting updates, 4 PM coffee breaks, and respectful leave requests.
7. **Education & Campus (ಶಿಕ್ಷಣ)**: Classroom etiquette, library directions, assignment deadlines, and notes.
8. **Bank & Public Services (ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್ ಮತ್ತು ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ)**: Form filling, Aadhaar verification, and token counters.
9. **Social & Small Talk (ಸ್ನೇಹ ಮತ್ತು ಮಾತುಕತೆ)**: Greetings, asking wellbeing (`ಊಟ ಆಯ್ತಾ?`), and Rajyotsava wishes.
10. **Quick Help & SOS Mode (ತುರ್ತು ಸಹಾಯ)**: One-tap emergency assistance, lost directions, police, and ambulance (108).

### 9. 🎙️ Real-Time Speech Capture Studio & Pronunciation Lab (ಧ್ವನಿ ಲ್ಯಾಬ್)
* **High-Accuracy Speech Capture**: Built for clients, learners, and newcomers to practice speaking authentic Kannada out loud into their microphone.
* **Dual-Engine Speech Recognition**:
  - Leverages HTML5 Speech Recognition (`kn-IN` locale) for instant spoken transcript generation.
  - Simultaneously captures raw microphone audio via `MediaRecorder` into an audio buffer.
* **Side-by-Side Audio Comparison (Pedagogical Excellence)**:
  - **"Play My Voice" (ನನ್ನ ಧ್ವನಿ ಕೇಳಿ)**: Replay your own microphone recording.
  - **"Play Native Model" (ಮಾದರಿ ಉಚ್ಚಾರಣೆ)**: Hear authentic Kannada spoken at normal (1.0x) or slow (0.7x) speed for direct audio comparison.
* **Syllable-Level Match Breakdown**:
  - Automatically highlights matched syllables in green and missed sounds in amber.
  - Provides culturally authentic feedback (e.g., *"ಸಕ್ಕತ್! ಅತ್ಯುತ್ತಮ ಉಚ್ಚಾರಣೆ!"* or *"ತುಂಬಾ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ! ಉತ್ತಮ ಪ್ರಯತ್ನ!"*).
* **Gamification & XP Rewards**:
  - +20 XP earned per successful practice attempt.
  - Streak counters and real-time session tracking across the platform.
* **One-Tap Practice Shortcuts Across the Platform**:
  - Tap the mic icon (🎙️) on **any visual pictogram card** to practice it instantly in the Speech Studio.
  - Launch speech practice straight from the **Giant Screen Mode modal** or any **situational phrase card**.

## 🏃 Quick Start Guide

### Option 1: 100% Offline Browser Mode (Zero Setup)
Simply open [`index.html`](./index.html) in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
- Works completely offline without an internet connection or web server!
- All phrases, builder items, soundboard letters, quizzes, and speech capture run locally.

### Option 2: Full-Stack Python Flask Server
```powershell
# 1. Install dependencies
pip install -r requirements.txt

# 2. Run the server
python app.py
```
Open **`http://localhost:5000`** in your browser.
